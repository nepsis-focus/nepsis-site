Privacy Policy

Nepsis · Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Who we are

Nepsis is a small project that helps people build their focus step by step. For any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at nepsis.info@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect only the email address you enter when you subscribe to the Nepsis newsletter. We do not ask for your name, photo, location, or any other personal details.

3. How we use your data

We use your email address to send you the newsletter, including focus tips and updates about new stages, and to confirm that you want to receive it (double opt-in).

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

4. Who processes your data

Newsletter emails are sent and stored through Brevo, an email service provider, which processes your email address on our behalf under its own privacy terms (brevo.com).

Anonymous visit statistics are collected through Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics provider. Umami processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy terms (umami.is). Umami does not use cookies and does not collect your email address or other personal details.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we remove it from the list.

6. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Unsubscribe using the link at the bottom of any email we send you.
  • Ask for access to the data we hold about you.
  • Ask for deletion of your email address by contacting us at the address above.
  • Object to or restrict how we use your data.

We will respond to requests within one month.

7. Cookies

This website uses Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they view. Umami does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and does not track you across other websites. It records only anonymous, aggregated information such as pages viewed, approximate country, and the referring site. Language preferences are not stored.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

9. Complaints

If you believe your data is not handled properly, contact us first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Greece, this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

This policy is a general template and not legal advice. Please review it before relying on it for a larger project.
