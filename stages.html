Stages

From 5 minutes to one hour of focus

Every stage builds on the one before. Move on only when the current one feels natural.

1

Start · 5 min

A small everyday task, like brushing your teeth. The goal is to finish something for sure.

2

Steady · 10 min

Ten minutes to focus and decide what you will do today.

3

Depth · 25 min

A bit more work: a small part of what you have to do.

4

Flow · 45 min

A small study session, without interruptions.

5

Mastery · 60 min

One hour of focus on your work. Close what you don't need.
