What it is

Small steps, every day.

You open your phone to check one thing and suddenly forty minutes have passed. Nepsis does not ask you to switch everything off. It asks for one small goal today: twenty minutes on your coursework or your job, with nothing else open.

What the research shows

In a 2026 Pew Research survey in the US, 70% of adults aged 18 to 29 say they spend too much time on their smartphone, compared with 53% of all adults. In a 2026 YouGov survey in the UK, 54% of 18 to 24 year olds say smartphones affect their concentration. Among those who tried to change their habits, 31% noticed better focus.

Gloria Mark, a professor at the University of California, Irvine, found that the average time people spend on one screen before switching dropped from about 2.5 minutes in 2004 to about 47 seconds around 2020.
