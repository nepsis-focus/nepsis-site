What it is

Small steps, every day.

You open your phone to check one thing and suddenly forty minutes have passed. Nepsis does not ask you to switch everything off. It asks for one small goal today: twenty minutes on your coursework or your job, with nothing else open.

How it works

Three simple steps

1

Start with a few minutes

A short focused session, for example 5 minutes, with no pressure.

2

Share your goal

Today's commitment joins the collective progress.

3

Build up slowly

A few more minutes each week, as far as you feel ready.

Stages

From 5 minutes to one hour of focus

Every stage builds on the one before. Move on only when the current one feels natural.

1

Start · 5 min

One short session and one question: what is the first thing I'm doing?

2

Steady · 10 min

The same time every day, with notifications off.

3

Depth · 25 min

One full work block followed by a short break.

4

Flow · 45 min

One uninterrupted session on a single task.

5

Mastery · 60+ min

Focus becomes a habit. For the task you care about, you no longer need effort to stay on it.

What the research shows

In a 2026 Pew Research survey in the US, 70% of adults aged 18 to 29 say they spend too much time on their smartphone, compared with 53% of all adults. In a 2026 YouGov survey in the UK, 54% of 18 to 24 year olds say smartphones affect their concentration. Among those who tried to change their habits, 31% noticed better focus.

Gloria Mark, a professor at the University of California, Irvine, found that the average time people spend on one screen before switching dropped from about 2.5 minutes in 2004 to about 47 seconds around 2020.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Nepsis?

An app that helps everyone gradually extend their ability to focus, with small daily steps.

Is it free?

Yes, the basic use is free.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need an email address to receive the newsletter, and you confirm it with one click.

Do I need friends to take part?

No. The shared goal is with people you don't know, and you can stay anonymous.

Who sees my data?

Only your email address, used to send the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing bad happens. Start again tomorrow, with no guilt and no streak to lose.
